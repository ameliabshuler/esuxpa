About Us

UXPA International advances the user experience field by advocating for industry standards, promoting human-centered design, and empowering professionals through education and community. In addition to UXPA International's annual conference, they host monthly, online webinars in English, publish a monthly newsletter, have a job board and mentoring program. Learn more here.

UXPA Spain is a local chapter of UXPA International, which aims to promote user experience in Spain through events, workshops, and networking activities. Our goal is to create a strong community of professionals and enthusiasts of user-centered design, fostering the exchange of knowledge and best practices.
